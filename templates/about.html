About Career Compass

Career Compass is a Final Year BSc Computer Science project that helps students explore suitable career options based on their education, skills, programming knowledge, interests and a short career assessment quiz.

How the recommendation system works

This project uses a simple, explainable weighted scoring algorithm - not a complex neural network or LLM. For every career, four factors are combined:

  • Skills (40%) - how well your current skill levels match what the career requires.
  • Interests (30%) - how many of the career's related interests you selected.
  • Assessment Quiz (20%) - points earned toward that career from your quiz answers.
  • Academic Background (10%) - based on your CGPA.
Important: Career recommendations are guidance only, based on the information you provide. They are not guaranteed predictions of your future career. The system suggests suitable career options based on your skills, interests and assessment results.
